LGA and main board

A local balancer measures the LGA and caps the chargers itself, without OCPP: what the building leaves free, minus the margin. Each car gets at least its minimum (6 A) in order of arrival while it fits, the rest is shared equally; the ones that don't fit wait. The CSMS's Smart Charging profiles still apply on top: the lower limit wins.
